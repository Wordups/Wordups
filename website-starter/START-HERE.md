# Start here

This is your website. You bring the ideas, words, and photos. Claude handles the
technical side: building pages, making it look right on phones, and publishing it.

You never need to write code. Just describe what you want, the way you would to a
friend who is good with computers.

## Your first conversation

Copy this, fill in what you know, and send it to Claude. It's fine to leave things
blank or say "not sure yet".

> I want to build a website. Here's what I'm thinking:
>
> - **What it's for:** (a portfolio, a small business, a blog, a shop, sharing a hobby...)
> - **Who it's for:** (clients, friends and family, people who love what I make...)
> - **What I want visitors to do:** (contact me, buy something, read my stories, book a session...)
> - **Pages I imagine:** (Home, About, Gallery, Contact...)
> - **The feeling it should have:** (cozy, bold, elegant, playful, calm...)
> - **Colors or styles I love:** (or websites I like the look of)
> - **A name or title:**
> - **What I already have:** (photos, a logo, writing, a social media page...)

Claude will ask a few follow-up questions, then build a first version you can look at.

## Things you can say anytime

- "Show me what it looks like right now."
- "Make the headings bigger and the background softer."
- "Add a page about how I got started. Here's what I want it to say: ..."
- "Here are some photos for the gallery." (attach them)
- "I don't love this. Give me two or three other options."
- "Undo the last change."
- "Save an idea for later: a newsletter sign-up."
- "It's ready. Publish it."

## Good to know

- Nothing goes live until you say it's ready.
- You can always go back to an earlier version, so experiment freely.
- Your words stay your words. Claude will ask before changing your writing.
